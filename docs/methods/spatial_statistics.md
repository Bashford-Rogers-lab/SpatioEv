# Spatial Statistics

SpatioEv includes Ripley and Moran families for point-pattern and feature
autocorrelation analyses.

- Ripley statistics measure clustering or co-localization at a spatial scale.
- Moran statistics measure spatial autocorrelation of continuous features.
- Cross-Moran statistics connect source and target feature programs.
- Local statistics identify cell-level or region-level spatial hotspots.

These methods are useful both as standalone spatial biology analyses and as
inputs to spatial pseudotime interpretation.
